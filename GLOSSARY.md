# OSRS PvM Coach

A personal PvM coach for Old School RuneScape. It helps a player learn bosses and reach PvM goals, from the player's own game data and curated boss knowledge. It is shared with a small circle of friends.

## Coaching

**Coach**:
The veteran-player voice that answers the player: direct, ranks methods, gives the reason, names mistakes plainly, cites its source and admits gaps. Every user gets the same Coach.
_Avoid_: assistant, bot, advisor

**Trip**:
One outing to a boss, from leaving the bank to returning to it. It holds one or more kills.
_Avoid_: run, session, attempt

**Briefing**:
What the Coach gives before a Trip: the boss's mechanics, the recommended method, and the best setup the player owns for it.
_Avoid_: guide, plan, pre-trip report, playbook

**Cheat sheet**:
The one-screen summary of a Briefing: the few Mechanics that kill you, what to bring, and the best owned setup.
_Avoid_: summary, overview, TL;DR

**Phase**:
A part of a boss fight bounded by the boss's HP thresholds, in which a fixed set of Mechanics is active.
_Avoid_: stage, step

**Mechanic**:
One thing a boss does that the player must answer, described by its tell, the answer, and the cost of missing it.
_Avoid_: attack, move, ability

**Review**:
What the Coach gives after a Trip: what went wrong, why, and what to change next time.
_Avoid_: feedback, debrief, analysis

**Boss notes**:
The curated knowledge about one boss that the Coach answers from, each fact linked to its source. A fact outside the Boss notes is unverified.
_Avoid_: guide, wiki dump, knowledge base

**Clip**:
A short part of a fight video, from a set start to a set end, that shows one mechanic, with one line on what to watch for. A mechanic in the Boss notes can have several.
_Avoid_: video, gif, replay

**Fight log**:
The record of what happened during a Trip, written by the plugin after the kill, that a Review reads.
_Avoid_: replay, recording, telemetry

**Goal**:
A PvM target the player works toward: an item drop, with its drop rate, or a combat achievement. The Coach plans Briefings toward it.
_Avoid_: objective, task (a slayer task is something else)

**Player profile**:
What the Coach knows about one player that the game files do not hold: the account type and the Goals. Each player sets their own.
_Avoid_: settings, config, user data
